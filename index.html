WHY ALUMINUM FISHING BOATS

What Makes an Aluminum Fishing Boat the Right Choice?

Aluminum fishing boats are the go-to choice for anglers who want a lightweight, low-maintenance hull that holds up season after season. Unlike fiberglass, aluminum won't blister or crack, resists corrosion in both fresh and salt water, and tows easily behind almost any vehicle — which is a big part of why jon boats, aluminum bass boats, and multi-species aluminum boats dominate lakes and rivers across the country.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Aluminum Fishing Boat FAQ

What's the difference between a jon boat and an aluminum bass boat?

A jon boat is a flat-bottomed, no-frills aluminum fishing boat built for shallow water, utility work, and duck hunting. An aluminum bass boat is more performance-oriented, typically with a livewell, casting decks, and a bigger outboard for covering water fast on tournament days.

Are aluminum fishing boats better than fiberglass?

Aluminum fishing boats are generally lighter, easier to tow, more resistant to corrosion, and lower-maintenance than fiberglass — they won't blister or crack the way fiberglass hulls can over time. Fiberglass boats often ride smoother in rough water, so the right choice depends on where and how you'll use the boat.

How much does an aluminum fishing boat cost?

Prices vary widely by size, brand, and features — a basic jon boat can start at a few thousand dollars, while a fully rigged aluminum bass boat with a large outboard can run well into five figures.
